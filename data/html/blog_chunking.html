Choosing a Chunk Size for RAG

Published on 2024-11-04

When we built an internal assistant that answers questions about our products and policies, the first decision was how to split our documents into chunks before creating embeddings.

Why chunk size matters

Small chunks, around 200 tokens, produce precise matches but often miss the surrounding context needed to answer a question. Large chunks, above 1,500 tokens, keep context but mix several topics, which makes the embedding less specific and wastes space in the prompt.

What worked for us

  1. Chunks of about 500 tokens with 50 tokens of overlap for long documents like manuals.
  2. One chunk per question and answer pair for the FAQ page.
  3. One chunk per row for spreadsheets with product data, keeping the column names in the text.

Conclusion

There is no universal best chunk size. Build a small test set of real questions, try two or three strategies, and measure how often the correct chunk appears in the top results.
