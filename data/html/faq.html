Frequently Asked Questions

What is your return policy?

Frames and sunglasses can be returned within 30 days of purchase if they are unused and in their original packaging. Prescription lenses made to order cannot be returned, but we remake them for free if the prescription was applied incorrectly.

How long does the warranty last?

All frames have a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Lens coatings have a 6-month warranty. Scratches caused by misuse are not covered.

How long does it take to make prescription glasses?

Single vision lenses are ready in 3 business days. Progressive and high-index lenses take 7 to 10 business days because they are produced by an external laboratory.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery within Salvador is free for orders above R$ 300. Orders to other cities in Brazil are shipped by courier and arrive in 3 to 8 business days.

Which payment methods do you accept?

  • Pix, with a 5% discount
  • Credit card, up to 10 installments without interest
  • Debit card and cash
