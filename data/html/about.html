About Lumen Optics

Lumen Optics is a family-owned optical store founded in 2012 in Salvador, Bahia. We started with a single counter in a shopping mall and today operate two physical stores and an online shop.

Our mission

We help people see better and feel confident with the glasses they wear. Every customer receives a free vision screening and personalized advice on frames that fit their face shape and lifestyle.
